How It Works

1

Bring Bottles

Collect clean and dry plastic bottles. Remove caps and crush lightly if possible.

2

Insert & Scan

Place bottles into the slot. The machine automatically verifies and counts each one.

3

Get Points

Points are credited instantly to your account or printed on a receipt.

4

Rewards!

Redeem for load, discounts, or donations. You helped save the planet too! 🎉

Our Mission

SmartBottle is a student-led innovation designed to reduce plastic waste in our communities. By making recycling convenient and rewarding, we aim to change habits and create a cleaner environment for the next generation.
